## PHASE 5 — Arrival (2031)

By mid-2031, five years out, the three goals should be met:

1. **"Get any job":** 4 years of tech-company digital marketing (intern → executive → senior), certifications, GMAT banked, possibly an MBA in progress on scholarship.
2. **"Start a business":** already done — Pack and Pass will have been running for ~3 years as a side business, ready to go full-time or keep as income while you do the MBA.

## The priority logic in one paragraph

**Skills before business, deadlines before everything.** The Aug–Dec 2026 window (internship portals + CAT/NMAT) is unrepeatable — protect it above all. The internship → tech job path is the engine: it pays you, trains you, and strengthens every scholarship application. Pack and Pass launches *after* the first job is secured (2028), because a marketing job at a SaaS company is literally paid training for running your own travel brand. Scholarships are structured as free options along the way — Path A if the job market disappoints, Path B (the default) if it doesn't. The MBA is a multiplier, never a blocker: the ₹2.76L UNITAR online backup means no path leaves you without one.

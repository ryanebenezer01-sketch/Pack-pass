The other minimum checks & the ticket total

HIP — Higher Intermediate Point

If an intermediate city-pair prices higher than your through O&D fare, use that higher fare. (Calculator #3 above.)

CTM — Circle Trip Minimum

On a circle trip (you loop back to the origin a different way), the total must be at least the highest one-way fare from the origin to any turnaround point.

BHC — Backhaul Check

On a one-way component, if an intermediate point's fare is higher than the destination's, you may be "back-hauling" past a dearer market — you're charged at least that higher intermediate fare.

TTL — the ticket Total

What the passenger actually pays = LCF (the constructed fare in local money) + taxes + fees + carrier surcharges (YQ/YR). The TTL line on a quote.

Order of build: construct the fare (TPM→MPM→EMS) → run the minimums (HIP · CTM · BHC) → sum components in NUC → convert with the ROE to LCF → add taxes/surcharges = TTL.
